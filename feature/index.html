Yo, I’m Nahoa, an LA-based artist and music content creator who shares album analyses (not ratings), artist spotlights, and live show reports. I’m passionate about discovering and promoting new music, especially hip-hop and jazz, as well as R&B, folk, and experimental. Many of my videos get excellent engagement and even go viral (regardless of how large the artist is), because people appreciate how earnestly I talk about music, and also I’ve just developed a knack for writing and editing engaging short-form content. There are so many dope projects and shows that just need to reach the right audience, which is why I help to highlight fellow artists and performance spaces. I’m currently majoring in music industry at UCLA, and I go to see live music every week; I’d love to see you, your artist, or your venue next!
